French vocabulary from textbook exercises. Click “Listen” to hear browser French TTS. IPA/source links are based on Wiktionary where available.

Pronunciation reminders: French stress usually falls near the end of the phrase. In l’homme, l’hôtel, and l’héroïne, the h is h muet. But le héros has h aspiré, so there is no elision.
